# Culture: cast and click lines (source draft)

Part of `docs/game/culture.md`. The shipped version is in `js/culture_data.js` (the code wins where it differs; cuts and rewrites were made while building). World content (walls, images, places, events, UI text): `docs/game/culture-world.md`.

## How to read this

**Two kinds of characters, two rules:**

- **Archetypes** (the bulk): invented names, no real person. Their lines are written by us in the voice of Crypto Twitter, so the jokes can be as sharp as we like. Each has 10 to 12 lines; a click picks one at random, never the same twice in a row. Add more any time.
- **Cameos** (a real public figure): they say **only what they really said** (source and year on the click card, small print). Lines are in `culture-cameos.md`; here only Satoshi's Shadow and The Old Bankers remain. Every cameo line has a **ship gate**: checked against the original before it goes live.
- **All dialogue plays only on click**, never because a player walked close.

**Source tags:** `[V]` found in a published source during research (secondary sources; the original post was not opened, so re-check the wording before shipping); `[K]` common community phrase, no single author; `[O]` given by the owner, not found by me; `[W]` written by us.

**Left out on purpose:** victims of scams and hacks as targets (jokes aim at founders, promoters and ourselves, never at people who lost money); anyone who has died, as a character.

**Not used:** Ansem (owner's call), Murad (only a token promotion found), anything about Mouch and Moyaki (Monad mascots, nothing reliable found).

**Where they stand:** each archetype also has a place and hours (they follow the real clock like the existing residents, `docs/game/world.md`). The existing 15 residents (Mika, Juno, Rex and 12 ambient) become this cast; their jobs map in brackets.
