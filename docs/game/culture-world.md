# Culture: world content (DRAFT for owner review)

Part of `docs/game/culture.md`. Characters and their click lines: `docs/game/culture-cast.md`. Nothing here is in the game yet.

Source tags as in the cast file: `[V]` found in a published source (secondary; recheck before shipping), `[K]` common community phrase, `[O]` from the owner, `[W]` written by us. Art rule: every image is **redrawn in our style** (`docs/art/art-direction.md`), never copied; no real person's likeness without their cameo gate; no third-party PFP collection art (BAYC, Punks, Milady...) without a licence check.

Not every wall needs a joke: a quiet place with one good plaque beats a loud one. Suggested density: one reference per ~10 m of walking, clusters only where people stop (lobby, elevator, bar).

## 6. The reference hunt

**What:** every plaque, image, prop and NPC line above gets an id. The first time you read or hear one it is "found"; My Chog shows a counter "References 17 / 120" and a grid of silhouettes that fill in. **No rewards that give power**; cosmetic titles and room decals only.

**Counters we can show:** plaques read, images found, NPCs met (23 archetypes + 7 cameos), lines heard, props used, events seen. Example titles: *Touched grass* (10 patches), *Few understand* (found 50), *Certified degen* (arcade 5 times), *Ser* (heard every Ser Gm line), *Bagholder* (stood by the exit 10 minutes), *Original Hodler* (visited Dec 18).

**Why it matters:** it is the tour, the reason to come back, and the screenshot ("I found 94 / 120"). Design owner-side: a daily rotating "wanted" reference ("find the pizza guy today") and a weekly set tied to events in §4.

**Data format** (planned, JSON in the client): `{id, kind, text, source, tag, place, hours, gate}`; `source` is shown in small print on cards for `[V]` items; `gate: true` blocks shipping until the owner confirms.
